Examine → reason about causes and consequences → resolve one adjustment

Bennett's restaurant example shows the move: pause an automatic reaction, ask what response can improve the situation, then act without needless hostility.

A daily feedback loop

Use the outward and homeward journeys differently.

→

Journey out

Train deliberate attention on one chosen subject.

←

Journey home

Review recent conduct honestly and prepare a better response.

Books can supply ideas, but they cannot perform this candid examination for you. The loop turns learning into lived adjustment.

Modern note: attention is trainable but not totally controllable, and persistent worry can have clinical causes. The practice is an exercise, not a moral verdict or substitute for care.
