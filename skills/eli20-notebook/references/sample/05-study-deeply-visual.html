Bennett's London example shows a habit of mind: ask what produced the familiar fact and what it will produce next. Ordinary work becomes a window into human behaviour and connected systems.

Choose a bounded specialty

Depth begins when the field becomes small enough to hold.

Modern European change
The French Revolution for twelve weeks

A limited period, subject, form, or creator gives each session a relationship to the previous one. Specialisation replaces random motion with cumulative understanding.

Serious reading

Do not measure only pages. Reserve half the session for thought.

45m

Read with effort

slow progress through the surrounding country

The best field is one you can continue exploring slowly. Bennett's hierarchy of genres is personal; the durable principle is to choose material that demands active thought.
